About

Pulse is a running estimate of what happens on Earth while you're reading this. Every counter starts at zero the moment the page loads.

I built it because I missed the old Poodwaddle world clock. It doesn't exist anymore, and I used to lose hours to it — there was something about watching the world tick over in real time that I've never found anywhere else. This is my attempt at that feeling. No agenda, no mission statement. Pure curiosity and self-indulgence.

Global numbers are usually published as annual totals — 67 million deaths a year, billions of tons of CO₂. Those figures are too big to feel. Split into seconds and tied to the time you've actually spent on this page, they turn into something you can sense.

Built by Cesar, in Paraguay — cesar@cesarcovarrubias.com

Sources & Methodology

Counters are not live feeds. Each stat is calculated from a published annual figure divided into a per-second rate, then multiplied by the time elapsed — so the numbers are modelled estimates, not measured events. Figures below are being migrated to the sources listed here; anything not yet cited should be treated as a placeholder.

  • UN World Population Prospects Population, births, deaths. The official UN estimates and projections, 28th edition (2024 revision), covering 237 countries.
  • Our World in Data Health, energy, CO₂, poverty and more. Offers a public Charts API with direct CSV/JSON access.
  • World Bank Open Data GDP, economic and development indicators. Free and open access.
  • U.S. Treasury — Debt to the Penny US national debt. Updated at the end of each business day; free REST API, no authentication required.
  • WHO Global Health Observatory Disease, mortality by cause, and global health indicators.
  • UNODC Data Portal Homicide, drugs, trafficking and criminal justice statistics.
